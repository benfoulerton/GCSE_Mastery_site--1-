## What I found when I investigated

- Cognito numbers its lessons by unit (e.g. 5.1, 5.2 ...). The catalogue had every lesson from x.2 upwards but **was missing the first lesson of most units** (e.g. 5.1 Homeostasis, 4.1 The Reactivity Series, 4.1 Atomic Structure & Isotopes). I found these by following each unit-ending lesson's "Next up" link.
- I only added a lesson as a video if its page showed a "Show timestamps" list (the video chapters). Pages without one are written notes only. I spot-checked this against a known video lesson (Specialised Cells: has timestamps) and a known notes page (Work Done: none).
- **67 Cognito lessons have no video** by that test (e.g. Titrations, Work Done, Friction, Culturing Microorganisms). They stay in the data but are not shown to students.
- I could not play any video. "Verified" here means the page opened, the title matched and the chapter list was read. I did not watch the videos, so relevance beyond title, chapters and spec section is not confirmed.
- Access: a Cognito page showed "2 of 5 free notes and lessons this week". Cognito limits free use and may ask for an account. The site no longer calls these "free, no account"; the limit is explained inside each video's Details.
- Links: every new lesson page was opened successfully by the fetch tool on 5 Nov 2026. The older records were opened in an earlier pass. I cannot make automatic link checks from this workspace, so a link could still break later.
